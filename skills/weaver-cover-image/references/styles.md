# Cover Style Gallery

## elegant
- **Palette:** soft coral, muted teal, dusty rose, cream. **Elements:** delicate linework, subtle icons, balanced composition. **Best for:** thought leadership, business.
## tech
- **Palette:** deep blue, electric cyan, violet, dark bg. **Elements:** geometric shapes, circuit motifs, data viz. **Best for:** AI, software, digital.
## bold
- **Palette:** vivid red/orange, deep black, bright yellow. **Elements:** strong shapes, dramatic contrast. **Best for:** opinion, warnings, CTAs.
## minimal
- **Palette:** black, white, one accent. **Elements:** max whitespace, single focal element. **Best for:** focused explainers, philosophy.
## warm
- **Palette:** warm orange, golden yellow, terracotta, cream. **Elements:** rounded shapes, friendly motifs. **Best for:** lifestyle, education.
## nature
- **Palette:** forest green, earth brown, sky blue, sand. **Elements:** plant/organic motifs, flowing lines. **Best for:** sustainability, wellness.
## playful
- **Palette:** pastel rainbow, bright pops, light bg. **Elements:** doodles, quirky characters, speech bubbles. **Best for:** tutorials, casual topics.
## retro
- **Palette:** muted mustard, teal, brick, off-white. **Elements:** vintage textures, bold retro type shapes. **Best for:** history, nostalgia, brand stories.
